# Ask

## Business Problem

Bellabeat is trying to achieve growth in the smart device wellness market by using smart device data to gain insights 
into consumer behavior and develop more accurate & targeted marketing strategies.

## Business Questions

1. What types of consumers are using smart devices and who should Bellabeat target?
2. How should Bellabeat market its products based on consumer usage patterns?
3. What types of products or features could be relevant based on consumer behavior?

## Why This Analysis Matters

This analysis can help Bellabeat identify important consumer behavior and usage patterns. These insights can then be used
to develop more targeted marketing strategies and support Bellabeat's growth.
