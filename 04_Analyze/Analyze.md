# Analyze

After cleaning and processing the data, several key patterns were identified.

## Key Findings

- Average daily steps were approximately 7,638, while average daily calories were approximately 2,304.
- Activity levels were highest during the afternoon and evening, with activity peaking around 6 PM.
- Higher-activity days were associated with lower sedentary time.
- Distance traveled had the strongest tested relationship with recorded calorie expenditure, with a correlation of
- approximately 0.645.
- Sleep duration and activity showed only a weak negative relationship, with a correlation of approximately -0.19.
- Weekday and weekend activity levels were broadly similar, with average daily steps of approximately 7,669 on weekdays
- and 7,551 on weekends.

## Overall Analysis

The analysis showed that user activity patterns were more strongly related to movement and timing than to sleep duration 
or whether the activity occurred on a weekday or weekend. These findings provide useful insights into consumer behavior 
that can support Bellabeat's marketing strategy.
