# Act

## Conclusion

The analysis provided Bellabeat with insights into consumer activity patterns, including when users are most active,
how activity relates to sedentary behavior and calorie expenditure, and how activity compares across sleep duration 
and weekdays versus weekends.

These insights can help Bellabeat better understand its target audience and develop more targeted marketing strategies.

## Recommendations

### 1. Target Lower-Activity Users

Use activity and sedentary-time insights to develop campaigns encouraging lower-activity users to gradually increase 
movement and reduce sedentary time.

### 2. Use Activity Timing

Use the observed afternoon and evening activity patterns, particularly the peak around 6 PM, as an opportunity to test 
targeted advertisements, reminders, or engagement campaigns.

### 3. Promote Walking and Movement

Use the positive relationship between distance traveled and recorded calorie expenditure to promote walking and increased 
daily movement as part of Bellabeat's wellness messaging.

## Limitations

The analysis is based on observational Fitbit data from a relatively small sample. The relationships identified should 
therefore not be interpreted as proof of causation.
